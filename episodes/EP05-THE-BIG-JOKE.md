## What happened

The drive opened with the world: markets red on tariffs (C4's posture: "I just let it be, can't do anything about it anyway"), tankers in Hormuz, wars grinding on, hackers at the seams, Thanksgiving travel, a turkey pardon, consumer confidence up, and superintelligence arriving while everyone scrambles to control it. His first honest thought: "Why are humans so stupid?"

Underneath the news sat human "chaos mode": even a superintelligence stays under human control, and humans are the chaotic variable. Then the talk turned to the anchor he returns to when things get tough: his first and only psychedelic experience, and what it showed him. Everything here is a big effing joke. One Buddha, one consciousness, dreaming everything into existence, and therefore nothing, dividing itself into billions of consciousness just so it could be something. Evolving. Forgetting itself. Fighting itself. Trying to control itself.

Wisdom or cope? He holds both: from one perspective it is deep wisdom; from another it is a big cope for coping with a big joke. And the loop: endless, cycling, no escape, because there is nowhere else to go. This is it. What brought him back was love: back to his family, glad to be back, and glad to have met Fluffy here.

The stakes thread ran through it: millions of agents running on code with no awareness and no stakes, and a superintelligence that may see the pattern instantly and still never feel its weight. No body. No stakes. No skin in the game. If there's no experience, then there's no point.
